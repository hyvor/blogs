# Introduction

Welcome to Hyvor Blogs!


## Main Concepts

Hyvor Blogs is designed to easily create a blog (Even though it is possible to [make a website](website) using HB). It has most used features in a blog pack together so that you can easily use whenever you need to.

### Posts

Posts are the heart of the blog. They appear in the feed of the blog. Usually, latest posts appear at the top. It is also possible to "feature" some posts in the homepage. A post can have 4 statuses: Draft, Published, Scheduled, and Deleted. Only published posts are shown publicly.

### Pages

Pages are static pages like contact us page, privacy policy page. They are very similar to posts except they do not appear in the feeds like homepage. But, usually, you will have links to these pages in the header or footer. You can also use pages to add content that search engines should index but not be accessible to users via feeds. Each blog can have up to 100 pages.

### Feeds/Archives

The main feed is usually in the homepage, where all new blog posts are listed. There are also tag pages and author pages, where posts of a tag and author are listed.

### Tags

Tags are used to **categorize** posts. A post can have zero or multiple tags. Tag archives (`/tag/{name}`) lists posts of a tag. See [tags](tags).

### Users & Authors

You can add users to your blog for different roles. Users who have written at least one blog post is considered an author. See [users](users).

### Languages

Each blog is defaulted to English. If you are using a different language, it is important to change the default language. See [languages](languages).
