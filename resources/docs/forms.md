# Forms

You may want to collect data from your visitors. Embedding a forms service is the easier way to do that.

### How to Embed Forms {#how}

* First, signup for a [forms services](#forms). They allow you to create custom forms as you like.
* Then, add the form embed code to your blog:
  * To add a form to a post or page, use [custom HTML/Twig blocks](writing#custom-html).
  * To add a form to a specific place in the blog, you will need to [edit the theme](themes#edit).

## Forms Services {#forms}

Forms are great for collecting feedback for your website. Here are some services for generating forms:

* [Google Forms](https://www.google.com/forms/about/)
* [Microsoft Forms](https://forms.office.com/)
* [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/)
* [Wufoo](https://www.wufoo.com/)
* [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/)
* [Formsite](https://www.formsite.com/)
* [Paperform](https://paperform.co/)
* [Zoho Forms](https://www.zoho.com/forms/)

We are not affiliated with any of these services. There are plenty of other forms services not mentioned here.
