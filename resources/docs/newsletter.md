# Newsletter

A newsletter is a great way to build an audience and share valuable information.

## Adding {#adding}

To add an email signup form to your blog,

* First, signup for a [newsletter service](#services)
* Copy the given HTML code.
* Paste it in **Console &rarr; Settings &rarr; Comments & Newsletter &rarr; Newsletter Signup Form Code**.

## Positioning {#positioning}

All Hyvor Blogs [themes](themes) are designed to have a place for the newsletter signup form. If you want to change this position, you will need to [edit your theme](themes#edit), and change the position of the newsletter [placeholder](themes-templates#placeholders). In your template files, find and cut this code `{{ _newsletter | template }}`, and paste it in the place you need the signup form to be.
