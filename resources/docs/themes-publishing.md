# Publishing Themes

Are you ready to publish your theme to our marketplace? There are two things to do before that.

## marketplace.yaml

Add a `marketplace.yaml` to your theme's root directory.

```yaml
theme_name: 
demo_url: https://demo.your-theme.com

```

Do not use the dev blog subdomain you use for development as the demo URL. You will need a separate blog for this.
