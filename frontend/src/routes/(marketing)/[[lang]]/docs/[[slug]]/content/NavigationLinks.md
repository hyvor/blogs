<h1 id="navigation">Navigation Links</h1>

Navigation links are shown in the header and footer of your blog. You can configure them in the **Settings &rarr; Navigation Links** section of the Console.

<h2 id="create">Create a Navigation Link</h2>

To create a navigation link,

1. Go to **Settings &rarr; Navigation Links**
2. Click the **Create Navigation** button
3. Fill in the details: Name, URL, Type
4. Click the **Add** button
