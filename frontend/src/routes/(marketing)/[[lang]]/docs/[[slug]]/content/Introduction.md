# Introduction

Welcome to Hyvor Blogs 👋.

We built Hyvor Blogs to make blogging easy for everyone. Hyvor Blogs bundles everything you need to start and manage a blog into a single platform. We try to make our platform as intuitive and user-friendly as possible, allowing you to focus on creating content.

<h2 id="get-started">How to get started?</h2>

Visit the [Hyvor Blogs Console](https://blogs.hyvor.com/console) to create a new blog. You will be asked to create a HYVOR account, and then an organization. You can have multiple blogs under your organization. All of them can be accessed and managed easily from the Console.

<Callout type="info" title="Self-hosting">
    Hyvor Blogs is open-source and can be self-hosted for free if you know your way around servers. In that case, <a href="/hosting">start here</a>.
</Callout>

<h2 id="features">Features</h2>

Hyvor Blogs offers a wide range of features:

- **[Powerful rich text editor](/docs/writing)** - You can write your posts in the built-in rich text editor. Easily add images, audio, rich media, code snippets, and more.
- **[Multi-language support](/docs/languages)** - Everything is designed to support multiple languages, allowing you to reach a wider audience. 
- **[Customizable Themes](/docs/theme)** - You can choose a theme from our theme library or create your own theme from scratch.
- **[AI Agent](/docs/agent)** - An intelligent assistant that can read your posts, provide suggestions to improve them. Of course, you can disable it anytime.
- **[Invite your team members](/docs/users)** - Give your team members access to your blog. Multiple roles with real-time collaboration.
- **[Suggestion Mode](/docs/suggestion-mode)** - Suggest edits to your team members' posts, discuss changes right within the editor, and collaborate effectively.
- **[Custom Domain](/docs/custom-domain)** - Host your blog on your own domain like example.com or blog.example.com.
- **[Sub-directory Hosting](/docs/subdirectory)** - Host your blog on a sub-directory like example.com/blog. Multiple integrations are available.
- **[Headless](/docs/headless)** - You can use Hyvor Blogs in a headless mode, allowing you to integrate it with any frontend framework or static site generator.
- **[In-built SEO](/docs/seo)** - Hyvor Blogs is built with SEO in mind. Most of the technical SEO stuff is handled by us.
- **[Hyvor Talk for Comments](/docs/hyvor-talk)** - Our commenting platform, Hyvor Talk, can be easily integrated into your blog. Comes with a complimentary license.
- **[Hyvor Post for Newsletters](/docs/hyvor-post)** - Our newsletter platform, Hyvor Post, allows you to send newsletters to your subscribers. Comes with a complimentary license.
- **[Integrations via Custom Code](/docs/custom-code)** - Add any other integrations, such as analytics, forms, or custom scripts to your blog.
- **[Console API](/docs/api-console)** - Programmatically manage your blog, including posts, users, and settings, using our API.
- **[Webhooks](/docs/webhooks)** - Set up webhooks to receive real-time notifications about events happening in your blog, such as new posts, etc.
