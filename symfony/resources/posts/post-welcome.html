Congratulations, and welcome to Hyvor Blogs! You now have a beautiful place to share your thoughts freely with the rest of the world. Here are a few customizations you can make to get your blog ready.

Custom Domain

By default, your blog runs on a subdomain of hyvorblogs.io, but we highly recommend setting up a custom domain for your blog.

Custom Theme

By default, your blog runs on the Hello theme. However, you can easily choose a new theme for your blog. Visit our themes page to find all available themes, and change your blog's theme from the Theme section in the Hyvor Blogs Console.

Each theme comes with its own configuration options (e.g., accent color), which you can change by opening the theme's config.yaml file.

Are you a web developer? If you'd like to build your own theme, check out our theme development guide.

Writing your first post

All ready? It's time to write your first post. All writing happens right inside our Console, which comes with a powerful editor and all the tools you need for blogging. You can edit this post, or delete it and start writing a new one. You can also save drafts, and publish or schedule them for later. Head over to our Writing & Publishing documentation to learn more.

What's next?

Hyvor Blogs has a lot more to offer than what's covered on this page. A good place to explore further is our documentation, where you'll learn about setting up multiple languages, customizing routes/permalinks, and more.
