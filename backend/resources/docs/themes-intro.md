## Publishing Themes

Once you have finished developing your theme, you can submit it to our marketplace (We will review it before publishing). It is important to understand versioning before doing so.

## Theme Versioning

All themes have a version number like `v1`, `v2`. There are no subversions (`1.1` or `1.1.1`). So, every release is a major release. You cannot submit subsequent patches or minor releases.

The reason is that we believe once a user selects a theme, they do not want to update it ever unless they are planning a UI change on their blog.
